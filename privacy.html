2. Short version

The App is an offline puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. The only personal data processed comes from the advertising software (Google AdMob) described below.

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores your best score and your settings (sound, music, vibration) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • IP address (which can indicate approximate location);
  • device and account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • user product interactions such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information such as launch time, crashes and performance data.

This data is used for serving and measuring ads, personalisation (where you consent or where permitted), analytics and fraud prevention. It is transmitted encrypted. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) on first launch. Ads that use personal data are only shown according to your choice. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy options. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); for strictly necessary ad delivery and fraud prevention, our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Sharing and international transfers

Advertising data is processed by Google and its ad partners. This may involve transfers to countries outside the EEA, protected by safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as described in Google’s policies. We do not sell your data.

7. Retention

We do not keep personal data ourselves. Retention of data processed by Google is governed by Google’s policies.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold your personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google. You can also contact us at hamzakesdeutsch@gmail.com and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, e.g. the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address, with the date above.
