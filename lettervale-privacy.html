Privacy Policy – Lettervale

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Who we are

Lettervale (“the App”) is published on Google Play under the developer name Motivia Studios and provided by Ramazan Karabakan, Christoph-Kolumbus-Straße 5, 1. OG rechts, 14089 Berlin, Germany (“we”, “us”). Contact: hamzakesdeutsch@gmail.com. Legal notice: Impressum.

2. Short version

Lettervale is a word puzzle game. It has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location, and we do not run our own servers that collect your data. Personal data is only processed by the advertising software (Google AdMob) and, if you make a purchase, by Google Play, as described below.

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores your game progress (current chapter and level, coins, bonus points), your settings (language, sound, music) and whether you bought “Remove Ads” locally on your device. This data does not leave your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads (interstitial ads and optional rewarded video ads) served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • IP address (which can indicate approximate location);
  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • product interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information such as crashes and performance data.

This data is used for serving and measuring ads, personalisation (only where you consent or where permitted), analytics and fraud prevention. It is transmitted encrypted. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. If you buy “Remove Ads”, interstitial ads are no longer shown; rewarded ads remain available only when you choose to watch them.

5. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) on first launch. Ads that use personal data are only shown according to your choice. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy options. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); for strictly necessary ad delivery and fraud prevention, our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The App offers optional in-app purchases (Remove Ads and coin packs). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details. The App only receives a confirmation of the purchased product so it can be delivered and restored. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

7. Game engine

The App is built with the Unity engine (Unity Technologies). The engine may process limited technical device information (e.g. device model, operating system version) needed to run the App and to report crashes, as described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

8. Sharing and international transfers

Data is processed by Google and its ad partners and, to a limited extent, by Unity. This may involve transfers to countries outside the EEA, protected by safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We do not sell your data.

9. Retention

We do not keep personal data ourselves. Retention of data processed by Google and Unity is governed by their policies. Data stored on your device is kept until you uninstall the App or clear its data.

10. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold your personal data ourselves, requests about advertising or payment data are best directed to Google. You can also contact us at hamzakesdeutsch@gmail.com and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, e.g. the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address, with the date above.
